Privacy Policy - OnlyCars

OnlyCars ("we", "us", "the app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

OnlyCars is not affiliated with Meta Platforms, Inc.

1. What Data We Collect

We collect data you provide while using the app:

  • Account data: email address, username, profile photo (if added), and user ID (UID).
  • Vehicle data: vehicle make, model, year, specification, and mileage.
  • History and costs: fuel logs (fuel amount, price, mileage), services (costs, descriptions, dates), parts and other expenses, events/collisions.
  • User content: photos uploaded by you (vehicles, documents, damages, etc.).
  • Sharing data: data included in shared garages (if you use sharing features).
  • Local device data: app settings (for example currency and preferences), notification state, achievements, and reminders.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used to:

  • operate the application,
  • save and display vehicle information,
  • track costs and history,
  • enable garage sharing (link/QR),
  • improve app performance and quality.

3. Sign-In Methods

You can sign in using Google Sign-In, Facebook Login, and Apple ID (for Apple devices). These services may provide basic data (for example email and name), used only for authentication.

Facebook Login

When you use Facebook Login, the app may receive basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture.

This data is used only for authentication and account creation.

The app does not post to Facebook and does not access additional Facebook data without your explicit consent.

4. Data Storage

Data is stored in secure third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication - user account data,
  • Cloud Firestore - app data,
  • Firebase Storage - photos and files.

Data may be stored on servers outside your country.

5. Data Sharing and Rights

We do not sell your data. Data may be shared only with service providers (Google, Firebase, Meta, Apple) or when you share a garage yourself (link or QR). Anyone with the link can view that shared data.

6. Data Retention Period

We keep data as long as your account is active. You can delete your data at any time in the app.

7. Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all data directly in the app. Deleting your account removes your profile, all garages, vehicle history, and all photos.

You can also request deletion by contacting us: onlycarssupport@gmail.com

Data Deletion Instructions

You can delete your account and all associated data directly inside the app.

You can also request data deletion by email at onlycarssupport@gmail.com.

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict processing, object to processing, and data portability.

To exercise your rights, contact us: onlycarssupport@gmail.com

9. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

10. Children

The app is not intended for children below the legal minimum age in your jurisdiction.

11. No Ads and No Tracking

We do not use user data for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

12. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated. The current version will always be available on this page.

13. Contact

If you have questions, contact us: onlycarssupport@gmail.com

By using the OnlyCars app, you accept this Privacy Policy.
